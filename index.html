Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard

Microsoft Power BI In Progress

How it is built

  1. Data source. Sales records from an Excel dataset, with fields such as Customer ID, Product ID, Category, City, State, Segment, Sales Region, Unit Cost, Unit Price, Total Sales, Total Profit and Total Discount.
  2. Power Query. Imported the Excel data into Power BI and transformed it so it was ready for analysis.
  3. Data modeling. Set up table relationships so slicers and charts filter the data correctly across tables.
  4. DAX measures. Created measures for the KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders and Total Discount.
  5. Visuals. Matched each chart to its question: a line chart for sales and profit by month, donut and pie charts for category share, and bar charts for ranking products, regions and categories.
  6. Interactivity. Month, State and Segment slicers and a Product Name filter let the viewer narrow the report, and a detail table shows the underlying records.
  7. Layout. Dark navy theme with KPI cards on top, charts in the middle and a short Key Business Insights box at the bottom.

E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

Microsoft Excel

How it is built

  1. Data source. E-commerce order records, 25 orders from January to March, with category, sales rep, order status, region, revenue, units and rating.
  2. Pivot Tables. Built separate pivots for revenue by category, revenue by sales rep, order count by status and revenue by month.
  3. Headline KPIs. Calculated Total Revenue, Total Units Sold and Average Rating and placed them in KPI boxes at the top of the dashboard.
  4. Charts. Turned the pivots into charts: columns for Sales Rep Performance, a donut for Order Fulfillment Status, columns for Revenue by Category and a line for the monthly revenue trend.
  5. Slicers. Added Shipping Status and Region slicers so the viewer can filter the analysis.
  6. Layout. KPI boxes and slicers across the top with the charts arranged below on one dashboard sheet.
